About PhonePlus Kenya

It started with a simple frustration: buying a phone in Kenya often felt more complicated than it needed to be. Too many unclear options, incomplete details, and uncertainty about what you were actually getting.

That’s why we created PhonePlus Kenya. Based in the heart of Mombasa Town and delivering nationwide, we set out to make finding your next smartphone clearer and more confident.

We carefully select both brand-new and quality Ex-UK phones, and we make sure you have the important details upfront — so you can compare, decide, and buy without second-guessing.

We’re not just selling phones. We’re helping people choose devices that truly fit their lives, budgets, and needs. Every phone on our shelves is chosen with care, and every customer interaction is guided by honesty and clarity.
