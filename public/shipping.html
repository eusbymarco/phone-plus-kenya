Shipping & Returns Policy – PhonePlus Kenya

Thank you for choosing PhonePlus Kenya. This policy explains how we handle delivery and returns so you know exactly what to expect.

Delivery Information

Mombasa CBD

Orders confirmed during business hours are delivered the same day.

Outside Mombasa (Nationwide)

Orders are delivered within 24 hours from the time of confirmation, through our trusted courier partners.

Delivery times may occasionally be affected by high order volumes, weather, or other factors beyond our control. We will keep you updated if any delay arises.

Order Handling

We aim to process confirmed orders quickly. Where possible, we also prepare and dispatch orders on weekends and public holidays.

Payment

We offer Payment on Delivery. You only pay when your phone arrives.

Delivery Fees

Any applicable delivery charges are clearly shown at checkout before you complete your order.

Where We Deliver

We deliver across Kenya.

We do not deliver to P.O. Boxes. Kindly provide a clear and accessible physical address (home, office, apartment, etc.).

Damaged or Missing Packages

If your order arrives damaged or incomplete, please contact us right away and keep the original packaging. We will assist you in resolving the matter with the courier.

Returns & Exchanges

Because we sell electronic devices, returns are only accepted for items that are defective, damaged on arrival, or significantly different from the description.

How to Start a Return

Contact us as soon as possible with your order details and a clear explanation of the issue. Photos or a short video help us assist you faster.

Return Conditions

  • Request must be made within 7 days of receiving the phone.
  • The device must be returned in its original condition with all accessories and packaging.
  • Proof of purchase is required.

Once we receive and inspect the phone, we will inform you of the next step — replacement, repair, or refund.

Warranty Coverage

Brand-new phones: Up to 12 months
Ex-UK phones: Up to 6 months

Please note that the exact warranty period may differ depending on the brand.

Warranty covers manufacturing defects only and does not include physical damage, liquid damage, or repairs done by unauthorized parties.

Refunds & Replacements

Approved refunds are processed using the original payment method or another agreed option.

Approved replacements are provided at no extra cost, subject to availability.

Return shipping costs are normally the customer’s responsibility unless the issue was caused by us or the phone was defective on arrival.

We recommend using a trackable courier for all returns.

Important Notes

This policy applies only to purchases made directly from PhonePlus Kenya.

We reserve the right to decline returns that do not meet the conditions above.

For any questions or assistance, reach out to us through Contact Us.
