We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. While no online system is completely secure, we continuously work to safeguard your data.

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

You have the right to:

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
