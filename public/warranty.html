Warranty Promise

Brand-new phones come with up to 12 months warranty, while Ex-UK smartphones are covered for up to 6 months. Please note that the exact warranty period may vary from brand to brand.

Brand-New

Up to 12 months

Ex-UK

Up to 6 months

Coverage

Warranty covers manufacturing defects only and does not include physical damage, liquid damage, or repairs done by unauthorized parties.

The exact warranty period is displayed and confirmed for the specific product where applicable. We do not assume every brand-new phone automatically has exactly 12 months of coverage.
